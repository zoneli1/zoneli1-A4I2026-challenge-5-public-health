How the Plan Self-Corrected (Pass 1 Rejected → Pass 2 Approved)

Race and ethnicity were strictly excluded from the ranking formula and verified afterwards against Fulton County baselines.

❌ Initial Naive Plan (Rejected)

Ranking purely by straight-line distance and uninsured rate clustered all 4 stops in North Fulton (Roswell / Alpharetta) within 0.5–1.6 km of each other, ignoring cross-border clinics in Gwinnett and skipping South/West Atlanta neighborhoods where 35–43% of households have no car.

✅ Revised Multi-Corridor Plan (Approved)

Accounts for zero-vehicle transit lock, discounts part-time/school-only clinic illusions, and enforces a minimum 4 km spacing across four distinct corridors. Reaches 88.8% minority residents and 42.6% zero-vehicle households without using race as an input.

What This Plan Cannot See (Data & Scale Limitations)

Public health datasets operate at four different resolutions that should never be blended into a single fake score:

1. Air Quality (PM2.5) Is a 12 km Regional Grid

PM2.5 values across all 326 Fulton tracts only vary from 8.98 to 9.81 µg/m³ (~9%). We do not rank individual neighborhoods by air quality; it is used only to stock asthma inhalers and spirometry county-wide.

2. Weekly Virus Trends Cover 4 Counties (HSA)

CDC emergency department trends for COVID-19, Flu, and RSV are reported for the 4-county region (Fulton, DeKalb, Clayton, Gwinnett)—not by census tract. We use them to time vaccine and rapid-test supplies.

3. Lapsed Federal HPSA Polygons vs. Synthetic Shortage Index

Federal `shortage_areas` has 0 active rows in Fulton because all 99 polygons are marked "Proposed For Withdrawal". Our `synthetic_shortage_tracts` index bridges this gap across all 326 tracts.

4. Part-Time & School Clinics Mask Unmet Adult Need

Several listed safety-net sites are school-only clinics, mobile vans, or open <20 hours/week. Our planner looks past these to measure distance to full-time brick-and-mortar clinics.
