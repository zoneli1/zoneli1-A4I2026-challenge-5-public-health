    How This Multi-Agent System Answers the 4 Challenge Judge Questions

    1. Does every agent in the diagram earn its keep?

    Yes—deleting any of the 8 agents removes either a BigQuery data domain or a required governance step: burden_analyst (PLACES/SVI + Google BigQuery MCP), access_analyst (distances, clinic hours, HPSA vacuum), context_surveillance_analyst (12 km PM2.5 & 4-county HSA guardrails), siting_planner (blinded operational optimizer), equity_and_rigor_auditor (exclusive holder of SVI Theme 3 & 95% CIs), clinical_accountability_writer (Parts 1–3 technical brief), community_outreach_writer (Part 4 bilingual 6th-grade flyers), and clinic_plan_synthesizer.

    2. How does state move between agents?

    Explicitly via ADK session.state keys (output_key and tool_context.state): parallel analysts write {burden_evidence}, {access_evidence}, and {regional_context}; inside the LoopAgent, siting_planner writes candidate_plan and equity_and_rigor_auditor writes audit_verdict, audit_feedback, and rejected_first_plan before setting tool_context.actions.escalate = True on Pass 2 approval.

    3. Do at least two agents have genuinely conflicting instructions?

    Two pairs conflict by design: (A) siting_planner (blinded to race, optimizes coverage) vs. equity_and_rigor_auditor (adversarial skeptic armed with SVI Theme 3 & 95% CIs); and (B) clinical_accountability_writer (hedged, cites CIs & grain flaws) vs. community_outreach_writer (warm, 6th-grade reading level, bilingual EN/ES, zero jargon).

    4. Can the system reject its own first answer?

    Yes: Pass 1 (13121011431, 13121011421, 13121011430, 13121011432) clusters all 4 stops within 0.46–1.58 km in North Fulton with overlapping 95% CIs and max 12.2% diabetes. The Auditor rejects Pass 1, forcing Pass 2 (13121007603, 13121010214, 13121007400, 13121002300) across 4 distinct corridors.
